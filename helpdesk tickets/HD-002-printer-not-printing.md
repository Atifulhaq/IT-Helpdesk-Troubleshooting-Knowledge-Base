# Practice Helpdesk Ticket 002: Printer Not Printing

## Ticket Information
- Ticket ID: HD-002
- Category: Hardware / Printing
- Priority: Medium (provisional)
- Status: Practice scenario
- Reported by: Example user
- Device: Windows computer and office printer

## Reported Issue
The user reports that documents are not printing from their computer.

## Initial Assessment
The cause is unknown. Possible causes include a connection issue, an offline printer, a paper jam, low supplies or a stuck print queue.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Ask whether the problem affects one document, one computer or multiple users.
2. Check that the printer is powered on and displays no obvious error.
3. Check the paper supply and any paper-jam warnings.
4. Confirm that the correct printer is selected.
5. Check the printer connection and whether it appears online.
6. Review the print queue for stuck or failed jobs.
7. Record findings before attempting a corrective action.

## Simulated Resolution
For this practice scenario, assume a document was stuck in the print queue. After following the approved procedure to clear the failed job and resubmit the document, printing worked again.

Note: This is an illustrative scenario, not a real incident or verified test result.

## User Confirmation
In a real support ticket, ask the user to confirm that the document printed before closing the ticket.

## Escalation
Escalate suspected hardware faults, persistent connection problems, recurring paper jams or issues affecting multiple users.

## Lessons Learned
- Establish the scope of the problem first.
- Check simple causes before escalating.
- Record each troubleshooting action and result.
- Follow organisational procedures before changing printer settings.
